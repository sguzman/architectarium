ABOUT THE RECONSTRUCTION

An interpretation, not an archaeological digital twin.

Published sources establish the broad composition of the temple: eight by seventeen exterior columns, six-column porches, separate east and west chambers, Ionic and Doric systems and 92 metopes. The model uses those sources while visibly distinguishing unsurveyed geometry. Sculpture and ancient color patterns are not reproduced. The optional Realistic materials mode uses original procedural stone veining and roughness maps, not scans of archaeological fabric or a verified historical color reconstruction. The Atmosphere setting adds illustrative warm haze and dust, not a reconstruction of ancient weather.
